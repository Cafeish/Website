How this saves data

Menu items, team photos, reviews, and colors/font you set here save to this browser. Come back later on the same computer/browser and they're still here. A different visitor, or you on a different device, won't see them — this keeps the site free and simple with zero setup.

If you outgrow this later and want changes visible to every visitor everywhere (e.g. once you have a team member updating the menu from their own laptop), that's a "connect a database" upgrade — it's a well-defined next step, just not needed to launch.
