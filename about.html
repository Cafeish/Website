About

Food the way it's actually eaten at home.

Cafeish started in a home kitchen in St. Louis, cooking dishes exactly the way they're made where they come from — no shortcuts, no softening the flavor for anyone.

What began as pop-ups for friends grew into catering across the city and surrounding areas. The mission hasn't changed: authentic global food, never westernized.

Authentic

Every recipe stays true to how it's made at home — we don't adjust spice, heat, or ingredients to fit anyone's comfort zone.

Sourced with care

We pay more for the real ingredient — saffron, cardamom, rosewater — because the difference is in every bite.

Community first

Built pop-up by pop-up, plate by plate, for the people of St. Louis and the flavors they didn't know were missing.
